## Why Skills?

Most AI adoption fails because it treats AI as a feature bolt-on rather than a product rethink. Skills flip that — they encode the *how* of professional work, not just the *what*. Every skill I ship is:

- **Domain-grounded** — built on real workflows, not generic prompts
- **Jurisdiction-aware** — because compliance isn't one-size-fits-all
- **Composable** — designed to work together, not in isolation
- **Open source** — expertise should be accessible
